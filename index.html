Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for PagePebble

Effective Date:

PagePebble ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how PagePebble handles your information when you use our Chrome extension.

Information We Collect & Store

PagePebble is designed with your privacy as a priority. The extension stores the following information solely on your local computer using Chrome's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`):

  • Note Content: The text and checkbox data you type into your PagePebble notes.
  • Note Position: The top and left coordinates defining where you place your note window on a specific webpage.
  • Note Size: The width and height dimensions you set for your note window on a specific webpage.

This information is always associated with the specific URL of the webpage for which the note was created.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected and stored locally by PagePebble is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To display your notes on the web pages you have created them for when you revisit those pages.
  • To remember and restore the last known position and size of your note windows for each specific webpage, ensuring a consistent user experience.
  • To manage the checked/unchecked state of checkboxes within your notes.

Data Storage, Security, and Transmission

All data created and managed by PagePebble is stored locally within your web browser's storage on your device. This data is NOT transmitted to, or stored on, any external servers. We do not have access to your notes or any of the data PagePebble stores.

The security of your locally stored data depends on the security of your computer and your Chrome browser profile.

Your Control Over Your Data

You have full control over the data PagePebble stores:

  • You can edit or delete the content of any note at any time.
  • Deleting all content from a note and saving it effectively removes the note for that specific page (it will no longer automatically reappear).
  • Using the "Delete Note" (trash icon) button within the PagePebble window will remove the note content, its position, and size data for that specific URL from local storage.
  • Uninstalling the PagePebble Chrome extension will typically result in the removal of all data that PagePebble has stored locally in your browser.

Permissions

PagePebble requires the following Chrome permissions to function:

  • storage: To save your notes, positions, and sizes locally.
  • activeTab & scripting: To display the note interface on the webpages you visit and to enable interaction when you click the extension icon. Our content script also runs on pages (as defined by ``) to automatically show your notes.

We only request permissions essential for the core functionality of the extension.

Third-Party Services

PagePebble does not integrate with any third-party services for data collection or tracking within the extension itself. The "Contact Us" and "Feedback" links on our promotional webpage may direct you to third-party services like Formspree for form submissions, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this promotional webpage. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
